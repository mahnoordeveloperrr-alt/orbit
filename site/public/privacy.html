Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Applies to the Orbitabs Chrome extension (Manifest V3) and its backend service (hosted Cloudflare Worker + D1; the Express + MongoDB build is the self-hosted alternative — same API, same rules). Open the Orbitabs web dashboard. · Web dashboard

Orbitabs is built local-first: your browsing sessions live in your own browser, and everything works fully without an account. This policy lists exactly what is stored, what (if anything) leaves your device, and how to delete it. If it isn't true of the code, it isn't here.

01

What stays ON your device (default)

Everything below is stored in chrome.storage.local inside your browser and is never transmitted anywhere unless you sign in AND explicitly turn on cloud sync (section 3):

  • Sessions you save: tab URLs, tab titles, window layout, tab-group names/colors/collapsed state, pinned/active flags.
  • Live Backup (auto-save) snapshots and recovery points used for crash recovery.
  • Settings (retention window, toggles, feature switches).
  • Markup annotations (drawings, highlights, notes) keyed by page URL.
  • Trash items — automatically and permanently purged after 30 days (configurable to 7).

Nothing here is readable by any website. Orbitabs never reads page content; the markup overlay only stores annotation data and never modifies the pages themselves.

02

Account data (only if YOU create one)

Signing in is never required. If you choose to create one:

Data Purpose Where
Name, email address Identify your account; send your verification link Our backend database
Password Stored hashed — bcrypt (self-host build) / PBKDF2-SHA256, 100k iterations (hosted service) — never in plain text Our backend database
Verification token Confirms you own the email (expires in 1 hour) Our backend database, deleted after use
Password-reset token Lets you choose a new password (single-use, expires in 30 minutes) Our backend database, deleted after use
Refresh token (one per device) Keeps you signed in without re-entering your password: 30-day sliding session, stored hashed, replaced on every use (a spent copy is rejected), removed at sign-out and account deletion Our backend database

We send exactly three types of email: the account-verification message, the password-reset message (only when you request it), and — only if you buy or cancel a paid plan — the plan-change confirmation (§7). No newsletters, no marketing, no tracking pixels.

03

Cloud sync data (off by default)

Cloud sync requires both an account and the toggle in Settings → Privacy & Data. When enabled:

  • What is uploaded: your saved sessions and Live Backup (auto-save) snapshot — tab URLs, tab titles, and window/tab-group structure. (Trashed session content is never uploaded.)
  • Deletion marks only: deleting a session on one device shares only its id and deletion time (a tombstone), so your other devices move their copies to their trash too; restoring lifts the mark the same way. Deleted session content never travels.
  • Version snapshots (optional feature): before each synced edit, the server keeps that session's previous state so you can roll back from History… (last 3 states on Free, 20 on Pro). Snapshots live with your account and are deleted with the session or the account.
  • Share links (only when YOU create one): sharing freezes a copy of one session (name, tabs, groups, tags, note) behind an unguessable link valid 30 days. Anyone holding the link can import that copy into their own account — nothing more. A share link never reveals your email or identity, can never change or delete anything of yours, dies when you re-share or delete the session, and carries a snapshot (later edits to your copy are not exposed).
  • Plan limit: the free plan cloud-syncs up to 100 saved sessions (enforced server-side; sessions beyond the limit stay on your device and keep working — local storage is never capped). No plan is sold today — see section 7.
  • Why: so your sessions are recoverable from your account on your other devices.
  • Where: our own backend (hosted: Cloudflare Worker + database D1; self-hosted option: Express + MongoDB), tied to your account id.
  • Controls: Local-only mode in Settings hard-disables all network calls, including sync. Sign out any time from Settings → Account.
04

What we NEVER do

  • No analytics, no trackers, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling, sharing, or renting of data — to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No cookies or fingerprinting on extension pages.
  • No reading of your page content for any purpose.
  • No changes to tabs you didn't act on — Orbitabs never reorders, groups, or closes tabs by itself.

The only network connections the extension ever makes are to its own backend, and only when an account feature you enabled requires it.

Server-side diagnostics — the one measurement that exists

The backend logs aggregate service health — request counts, response codes, latency and error rates — so outages and sync failures can be detected and fixed. These are ordinary server operational logs: no page content, no tab URLs, no analytics identifiers, and nothing that follows you across sites. The extension itself performs no analytics of any kind.

05

How to delete everything

  • Local data (instant, no account): Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete all local data.
  • Cloud account + cloud data: Settings → Account / Sync → Delete account & cloud data (password confirmation required). Permanently removes your account and every cloud session.
  • Trash: deleted sessions are held for 30 days (or 7, if configured), then purged automatically.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all local data from Chrome.
06

Security

  • Passwords: bcrypt hashing (cost factor 10) on the self-host build; PBKDF2-SHA256 with 100,000 iterations on the hosted service.
  • API access: signed JWTs (7-day expiry) plus rotating refresh tokens (30 days, one per device, stored hashed; a replayed spent token is rejected), rate-limited authentication (25 requests / 15 minutes / IP), explicit CORS origin allow-list, baseline security headers, HTTPS support with optional HTTP→HTTPS enforcement and HSTS.
  • Free-plan sync limits and every write rule are enforced on the server — the extension never decides its own permissions.
  • Deleted accounts are invalid immediately: a deleted account's tokens are rejected and all of its cloud rows are removed — sessions, deletion marks and refresh tokens alike.
